Selected Work

A selection of projects showcasing my expertise in robotics, SLAM, and autonomous systems.

3D Lidar SLAM @ Lionsbot

Engineered a drift-free 3D Lidar SLAM system for large-scale indoor environments, enabling autonomous navigation for commercial cleaning robots.

Visual Inertial Odometry @ NTU

Deployed VIO and SLAM algorithms on Micro Aerial Vehicles with Nvidia Jetson TX2, improving localization in GPS-denied environments.

Autonomous Driving Simulator

Built a simulation environment for testing perception, localization, and control algorithms for self-driving vehicles.
