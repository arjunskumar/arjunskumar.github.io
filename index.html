Abstract

I build and lead the estimation and autonomy stacks behind mobile robots: 3D LiDAR SLAM, factor-graph optimization and localization, now running on 1,800+ commercial robots worldwide. My work takes a problem from the whiteboard (a cost function, a noise model, a proof that it converges) through to fleet deployment, and I lead the engineering teams and cross-functional work needed to make it hold up in production. Over 8+ years this has spanned warehouse forklifts, commercial cleaning robots, micro aerial vehicles and edge perception.

Keywords: factor graphs, SLAM, state estimation, sensor fusion, GTSAM / iSAM2, LiDAR odometry, ROS 2, modern C++
